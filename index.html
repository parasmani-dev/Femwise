How it works

Built for real-world use.

A Flutter app on your phone talks to a small set of focused services. Accounts and logs live in MongoDB, food knowledge lives in a Neo4j graph, and the pose model never leaves the device.

On your phone
FemWise appFlutter, Android. Cycle calendar, daily logs, food scanner and guided routines.
Label readingGoogle ML Kit text recognition runs on-device.
Pose matchingMoveNet (TensorFlow Lite) compares your posture locally. No video leaves the phone.
Secure sessionSign-in token kept in encrypted storage.
FemWise services
Account APIFastAPI. Sign-up, sign-in, profile and daily logs behind bearer sessions.
Label analysis APITurns label text into an ingredient list and a PCOS verdict.
Meal analysis APIMaps a dish photo and name to its ingredients and rates them.
Data and AI
MongoDBAccounts, profiles and daily logs. Passwords hashed with Argon2.
Neo4jKnowledge graph of ingredients, their effects and healthier swaps.
ChromaDBFast lookup from dish name to typical ingredients.
Gemini and OllamaExtract ingredients from text and write the plain-language verdict.

App

Flutter and Dart, with Google ML Kit for label reading and a TensorFlow Lite MoveNet model for pose estimation.

Backend

FastAPI services. MongoDB stores accounts, profiles and daily logs. Neo4j holds the ingredient knowledge graph and ChromaDB powers dish lookup.

AI

Gemini writes the nutrition verdicts, Ollama extracts ingredients from label text, and Hugging Face models structure scraped nutrition data.

Security

Passwords hashed with Argon2, short-lived bearer sessions, and no database credentials ever shipped inside the app.
